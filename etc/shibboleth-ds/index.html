Welcome to Cosmic Explorer!

For the Public

People who are not members of the Cosmic Explorer Consortium can view the most recent or search for public documents in the Cosmic Explorer Document Control Center.

Information about joining the Cosmic Explorer Consortium is available on the Cosmic Explorer web site.

People joining the consortium (and existing members) should select an identity provider to login

The first time that you log in, you should pick from the list or enter an or organization name. Your previous choice(s) will be suggested on future visits, or you can select a different provider from the list below. The U.S. indentity providers listed below are those that federate with InCommon Research and Scholarship service. International identity providers are federated through eduGAIN.

Which provider should I choose?

  • If your home institution is available in the list below, select that provider.
    • Note that some indentity providers listed do not release all of their metadata to InCommon Research and Scholarship. If you encounter an error, please use one of the backup identity provides below.
  • If your home institution is not listed and you have a LIGO.org credential, then you can select the LIGO.org identity provider and log in with your LIGO.org credentials. Information on managing these credentials is available at my.ligo.org.
    • Note the KAGRA identity provider does not currently release their metadata to InCommon Research and Scholarship so you should use your institutional credentials, a LIGO.org credential, or your ORCiD.
  • If your institution is not listed and you do not have LIGO.org credentials then you can select the ORCiD identity provider and log in with your ORCiD credentials. If you do not have an ORCiD, you can obtain one by visiting the ORCiD registration page.

The identity selector will appear below. It may take a few seconds to gather the information from all available identity providers.
